Adding Responsa to your Google profile

Two minutes, no password, nothing installed. You give us manager access, and you can take it away again at any moment.

First: is your profile claimed?

Plenty of businesses show up on Google Maps without anyone having claimed the listing. Google created it, but nobody controls it — which means nobody can reply to reviews, including us. Check before you do anything else.

  1. Search for your business on Google Maps

    Use the business name and your city, the way a customer would.

  2. Look for "Claim this business"

    If you see that link, the profile is unclaimed. Select it, then "Manage now", and follow Google's verification steps. Verification can take a few days, so start it now.

  3. If you don't see it, you're set

    No claim link means the profile already belongs to someone. If that someone is you, carry on below.

Someone else claimed it? It happens — a former manager, an agency, a previous owner. Google has a request-ownership process for exactly this, and it's slow but it works. Tell us and we'll walk you through it.

Then: add us as a manager

Do this from a computer if you can — the menus are easier to find than in the mobile app.

  1. Open your Business Profile

    Search your business name while signed in to the Google account that owns it, or go to your profile in Google Maps.

  2. Go to More → Business Profile settings → People and access

    Those are Google's exact menu labels.

  3. Select "Add" at the top left

    A panel opens asking for an email address.

    Screenshot to be added
  4. Enter the Responsa email address

    We send you the exact address when you sign up — it's in your welcome email, so copy it from there rather than typing it.

  5. Under "Access", choose "Manager"

    Manager, not Owner. Manager is all we need, and it keeps you in full control of the profile.

  6. Select "Invite"

    We accept, and that's the setup finished. You'll see your first draft replies within a few days.

What manager access actually allows

Worth being straight with you: Google's Manager role is broader than just replying to reviews. Here's the real picture.

Can they… Manager (us) Owner (you)
Reply to reviews YesYes
Edit business info, hours, photos YesYes
Add or remove other people NoYes
Delete the profile NoYes
Remove our access NoYes

So a manager can technically edit your business details. We only ever reply to reviews. We don't touch your hours, photos, or description, and we can't remove you or delete anything. You can revoke us in the same menu you used to add us.

Removing our access

More → Business Profile settings → People and access, select our entry, then Remove. It takes effect immediately and you don't need to tell us first.

Common questions

Do you need my Google password?

No, and you should never give it to anyone. The invite works through Google's own permissions system — you're granting access to our account, not sharing yours.

Will replies go out without me seeing them?

No. Every reply is drafted, sent to you, and only published once you approve it. That's the whole workflow.

What if I don't like a reply?

Reject it, or tell us what to change. Corrections feed back into how we write for you, so the same problem shouldn't come up twice.

Can I stop at any time?

Yes. Remove our manager access and we're out — no notice period, nothing to cancel. Replies already published stay published, since they're yours.
