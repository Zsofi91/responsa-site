Privacy

What we collect, why we have it, who else sees it, and how to make us delete it. Last updated 19 July 2026.

Draft — not yet reviewed by a lawyer. This page describes our actual data handling honestly, but it has not been checked by a legal professional and the operator's registered details are still placeholders. Have it reviewed before relying on it.

Who we are

Responsa is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [REGISTERED ADDRESS], Hungary. For anything on this page, write to [CONTACT EMAIL]. We're the data controller for the information described below.

What we collect

When you fill in the form

  • Your name, email address, and phone or WhatsApp number
  • Your business name and its Google Maps link
  • Any menu, price list, or service document you upload or link
  • Your preferred language, and anything you tell us about your style

Once you're a customer

  • Your Google reviews, including the reviewer's public display name, star rating, and review text
  • The replies we draft, and whether you approved or rejected them
  • A record of what we published and when

We don't use cookies for tracking, we run no analytics, and there are no advertising trackers on this site. The form is embedded from Tally, which sets what it needs to work.

Why we have it

  • To provide the service — we can't write replies in your voice without your reviews and your service list. Legal basis: performance of a contract.
  • To contact you — about drafts awaiting approval, and about your account. Legal basis: performance of a contract.
  • To answer enquiries — if you fill in the form but never become a customer. Legal basis: legitimate interest in replying to you.

Who else sees it

We don't sell your data and we don't share it for advertising. We do rely on these providers to run the service:

Provider What reaches them
GoogleYour reviews and our published replies. We access your Business Profile as a manager.
TallyEverything you type into the onboarding form, including uploaded files.
Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude The text of a review and your style rules, sent so a draft reply can be written. This includes the reviewer's first name where they used one.
TelegramDraft replies sent to you for approval.

On the AI part, plainly: review text written by your customers is sent to a language model to draft a reply. Those reviews are already public on Google, but we think you should know it happens rather than find out later.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that go nowhere: deleted within 12 months.
  • Customer contact and business details: for as long as you're a customer, then 12 months.
  • The log of published replies: kept while we work together, because it's what stops us replying to the same review twice.
  • Replies already published on Google stay there — they belong to your business profile, and only you can remove them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to how we're using it. Write to [CONTACT EMAIL] and we'll respond within 30 days.

You can also revoke our access to your Google profile yourself at any time, without asking us — the setup guide explains how. That stops all processing immediately.

If you think we've handled your data badly, you can complain to the Hungarian data protection authority (NAIH).

How we hold it

Customer records sit in a private file on a server we control, readable only by the account that runs the service. We don't keep your Google password, because we never have it — access works through Google's own permission system and you can withdraw it yourself.
